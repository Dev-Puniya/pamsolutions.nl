How Our Recruitment Process Works

We follow a rigorous and transparent process to ensure every hire meets both employer requirements and legal obligations.

  1. Share Your Hiring Needs – Provide the job role, qualifications, skills and salary range. The more detail you provide, the better we can match candidates.
  2. We Source & Pre-Screen – We search for qualified, pre-verified candidates and conduct initial screenings.
  3. Employer Documents – We help prepare offers, contracts and permits per local laws.
  4. Visa & Embassy Support – We coordinate applications, appointments and file preparation.
  5. Arrival & Onboarding Support – From airport pickup to accommodation setup and workplace transfers, we ensure a smooth arrival and integration.

Why This Process Matters

By following a compliance-first approach, we ensure that every recruitment aligns with EU labour and immigration laws. Our diverse candidate pool undergoes thorough pre-screening for qualifications and legal documentation, giving employers peace of mind and workers a solid foundation upon arrival. Post-arrival services such as accommodation support and local registration help candidates settle quickly.

Recruitment Best Practices

To continually improve our services, we study industry best practices from established recruitment firms. A typical cooperation model involves four key steps: (1) employers share their hiring needs through an inquiry form; (2) a tailored offer is prepared covering job requirements, schedules, accommodation and legal documentation; (3) recruitment specialists manage the selection process from advertisements to interviews; and (4) arrival and onboarding are organised so workers start on time. By adopting these proven methods, PAM Solutions ensures our recruitment process remains efficient, compliant and client-focused.

Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy (GDPR – Netherlands): PAM Solutions (Amsterdam, Netherlands) is the data controller for recruitment services in the EU. We process only what is necessary to evaluate applications and provide staffing (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)(f)). Typical data includes contact details, CV, work history, right‑to‑work documentation and client contact details. We retain data only as long as needed for recruitment or legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. You can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object by emailing privacy@pamsolutions.nl. You may lodge a complaint with the Dutch DPA (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
