Privacy Policy (GDPR – Netherlands)

PAM Solutions (Amsterdam, Netherlands) is the data controller for recruitment services in the EU. We process only what is necessary to evaluate applications and provide staffing (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)(f)). Typical data: contact details, CV, work history, right-to-work documentation, client contact details. We retain data only as long as needed for recruitment or legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. You can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object by emailing privacy@pamsolutions.nl. You may lodge a complaint with the Dutch DPA (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

How Our Recruitment Process Works

We follow a rigorous and transparent process to ensure every hire meets both employer requirements and legal obligations. This step‑by‑step approach is inspired by our recruitment partner’s streamlined methodology.

  1. Share Your Hiring Needs – Provide the job role, qualifications, skills and salary range. The more detail you provide, the better we can match candidates.
  2. We Source & Pre‑Screen – Our team searches for qualified, pre‑verified candidates and conducts initial screenings to confirm experience and documentation readiness.
  3. Employer Prepares Job Documents – Once candidates are selected, we help prepare job offer letters, employment contracts and work permit documentation according to local laws.
  4. Visa & Embassy Support – We handle visa application steps, schedule embassy appointments, prepare required files and track progress.
  5. Arrival & Onboarding Support – From airport pickup to accommodation setup and workplace transfers, we ensure a smooth arrival and integration for every worker.

Why This Process Matters

By following a compliance‑first approach, we ensure that every recruitment aligns with EU labour and immigration laws. Our diverse candidate pool undergoes thorough pre‑screening for qualifications and legal documentation, giving employers peace of mind and workers a solid foundation upon arrival. Post‑arrival services such as accommodation support and local registration help candidates settle in quickly.

Recruitment Best Practices

To continually improve our services, we study industry best practices from established recruitment firms. Successful agencies are known for fast and effective solutions, strong communication networks and tailored staffing services. A typical cooperation model involves four key steps: (1) employers share their hiring needs through an inquiry form; (2) a tailored offer is prepared covering job requirements, schedules, accommodation and legal documentation; (3) recruitment specialists manage the selection process from advertisements to interviews; and (4) arrival and onboarding are organised so workers start on time. By adopting these proven methods, PAM Solutions ensures our recruitment process remains efficient, compliant and client‑focused.
