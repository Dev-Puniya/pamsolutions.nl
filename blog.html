Europe Jobs, Work Permits & Visa Guides

Original, country‑specific guidance for Hotel/Hospitality, Truck/HGV, IT & Technical, Construction, Warehouse/Production and Agriculture/Greenhouse roles across Europe.

🏨 Hotel & Hospitality

Malta – Hotel, Kitchen & Housekeeping

Salary €1 000–€1 400 · Full work permit + residence

Roles include kitchen helper, steward, room attendant, and cleaners. Employers sponsor work permits; English is widely used. Learn documents from India, timelines and onboarding basics.

Ireland – Hospitality & Support Staff

Salary €1 700–€2 200 · English speaking

Front/back‑of‑house, housekeeping and kitchen roles. Clear English advantage, structured permits and employer‑led onboarding.

🚛 Truck / HGV Drivers

Germany – Work Permit & CPC (Code‑95)

€2 200–€2 800 + allowances · Updated Nov 2025

Routes, employer sponsorship, licence exchange, tachograph card, and residence visa steps for non‑EU professionals.

Netherlands – IND, MVV & BSN

€2 300–€3 000 + international bonuses

Employer permits, MVV/residence, BSN registration, Dutch/EU road rules and CPC completion options.

💻 IT & Technical

Ireland – IT Support & Systems Roles

€2 000–€3 000 · English required

Service desk, system admin and junior network roles; strong English communication and vendor certifications help.

Romania – Technicians & Field Engineers

€1 200–€1 800 · Growing demand

Datacentre hands‑and‑eyes, NOC support and field techs; residence/TRC steps and employer process.

🏗️ Construction & Skilled Trades

Romania – Permit & TRC for Trades

€1 000–€1 400 + housing

Welders, painters, electricians and helpers; employer‑led permit, embassy visa, arrival and onboarding.

Slovakia – Skilled Labour & Fit‑Out

€1 200–€1 800 · EU compliance

Fitters, carpenters, masons and machine operators; Schengen residence route with employer sponsorship.

📦 Warehouse & Production

Lithuania – Logistics & Packaging

€900–€1 200 · Housing often included

Pick/pack, scanning and quality checks; employer permit flow and shift allowances.

Latvia – Factory & Forklift Roles

€1 000–€1 300 · Training available

Assembly line and MHE roles; paid training, medical tests and safety inductions.

🌿 Agriculture & Greenhouse

Netherlands – Greenhouse Workers

€5–€6/hour · Transport + housing

Tomato/cucumber picking, packing, hygiene and quality requirements; contract lengths and shift patterns.

Spain – Seasonal Harvest & Packing

€1 000–€1 200 · Seasonal contracts

Regional campaigns, visa steps, accommodation types and worker protections during peak seasons.

🇪🇺 Southern & Eastern Europe

Bulgaria – Food Production & Textiles

€900–€1 100 · Residence card route

Factory roles with employer permits and residence processing; basic language and medical requirements.

Italy – “Decreto Flussi” Seasonal & Construction

€1 100–€1 400 · Annual quotas

Seasonal and non‑seasonal quotas for agriculture and construction; timings, sponsor duties and renewals.
