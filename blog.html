European Recruitment, Visa & Job Market Insights

Explore recruitment pathways and visa rules for logistics, construction, production, and agriculture professionals across the EU.

🚛 Truck Drivers in Europe

Germany – Work Permit & Visa Route for Drivers

Updated Nov 2025 · Average €2,200–€2,800/month

Germany remains the EU’s largest logistics market. Learn about Code-95 certification, long-term residence routes, and how non-EU drivers can work legally under employer sponsorship.

Netherlands – IND Work Permit & BSN Registration

Updated Nov 2025 · Salary €2,300–€3,000/month

Understand Dutch IND procedures, MVV visa, and residence requirements for professional HGV drivers joining EU fleets.

🏗️ Construction & Skilled Trades

Romania – Construction Worker Permit Process

Salary €1,000–€1,400/month + housing

Romania actively hires welders, painters, electricians and helpers from Asia. Employers manage full work permits and TRCs for 1–2 years.

Slovakia – Skilled Labor & Engineering Jobs

Salary €1,200–€1,800/month

Learn how Slovak companies sponsor mechanical fitters, machine operators, and carpenters under EU compliance programs.

📦 Warehouse & Manufacturing Roles

Lithuania – Logistics & Packaging Jobs

Salary €900–€1,200/month

Warehouse assistants and packaging staff are in strong demand. Employers provide accommodation and in-country work permits.

Latvia – Factory & Supply Chain Staff

Salary €1,000–€1,300/month

Opportunities for forklift drivers and assembly line workers. Includes paid training and medical insurance options.

🌿 Greenhouse & Seasonal Jobs

Netherlands – Greenhouse Workers & Pickers

Salary €5–€6/hour · Accommodation included

Netherlands and Spain regularly hire greenhouse workers for tomato, cucumber, and strawberry farms under seasonal contracts.

Spain – Agricultural Helper & Harvest Jobs

Salary €1,000–€1,200/month

Spain’s southern regions employ thousands of foreign pickers and packers under legal seasonal visas. Learn about work conditions and contract lengths.

💻 Technical & Hospitality Careers

Malta – Technicians & Hotel Staff

Salary €1,000–€1,400/month

Malta hires maintenance technicians, kitchen helpers, and cleaning staff with full work permits. Learn the visa and accommodation process.

Ireland – IT Support & Skilled Workers

Salary €2,000–€3,000/month

Ireland continues to recruit international candidates for IT, health care, and logistics. English-speaking environment and EU permits simplify relocation.

🇪🇺 Other Emerging Destinations

Bulgaria – Industrial & Food Factory Jobs

Salary €900–€1,100/month

Bulgaria offers roles in food production and textiles. Employers manage full residence card processes.

Italy – Seasonal Permits & Construction Support

Salary €1,100–€1,400/month

Italy’s “Decreto Flussi” scheme welcomes foreign workers in agriculture and construction. Applications open each year under quota permits.
